            <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
            <p><em>Effective date: September 10, 2026</em></p>

            <p>This Privacy Policy describes how <strong>For Science: Field Kit</strong> (the "App") handles your information. The App is developed by Allie Ogden ("we," "us," or "our").</p>

            <h3>No Data Collection</h3>
            <p>For Science: Field Kit does not collect, transmit, or store on any server any personal information or usage data. The App stores game session data &mdash; such as whether you won or lost, remaining time, and game setup details like difficulty settings and player count &mdash; in a local database on your device only.</p>

            <p>The App does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services, and it does not require you to create an account or sign in.</p>

            <h3>Children's Privacy</h3>
            <p>The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, because it does not collect information at all.</p>

            <h3>Support Communications</h3>
            <p>If you choose to contact us for support or with questions, such as by emailing <a href="mailto:allie.ogden.apps@gmail.com">allie.ogden.apps@gmail.com</a>, we will receive whatever information you include in that message (for example, your email address and the contents of your email). This is separate from the App itself, which does not collect or transmit any information as described above.</p>
            <p>We use this information only to respond to your request, and we retain it only as long as needed to do so. Because this correspondence is handled through Google's email service, it is also subject to <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google's Privacy Policy</a>.</p>

            <h3>Changes to This Policy</h3>
            <p>If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.</p>

            <h3>Contact</h3>
            <p>If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at <a href="mailto:allie.ogden.apps@gmail.com">allie.ogden.apps@gmail.com</a>.</p>
